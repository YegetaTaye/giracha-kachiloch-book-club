# Surface brief: Giracha Kachiloch site (all routes)

Scope: home, /guests, /books, /support, shared header and footer. Visitor mode: Persuade (home, support); Experience-leaning on /guests and /books, inside the same world.

Audience and job: locals deciding whether to come (primary), writers and poets, supporters, diaspora. The one action is joining the Telegram channel to hear about the next night. Proof is the real room: hero video, venue photo, event posters, guest portraits, shelf sheets.

Constraints: no confirmed next date, entry price or email; no invented times per part of the night; coffee is a side element. Amharic leads, English complete.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the evening's printed programme. It shows what a night is (a guest writer in discussion with the audience, members reading their own work, songs) as a running order in the posters' colours. It refuses the hero-photo plus card-grid events page.

OWN-WORLD: Espresso ink field (#2b1b12) with parchment sheets (#f3ece1) for reading passages. Terracotta (#7a2e1d) marks the Telegram action and the closing band. Gold (#b8863b) and deep gold (#7f5a1e) carry rules and info-strip blocks. The palette was restored to the house's warm colours at the user's request on 2026-10-21, replacing the posters' navy and orange. Type is heavy condensed Noto Sans Ethiopic and Archivo for display, plain widths for body. Text rides 2px rules. Photos sit in rounded panels. No card boxes.

STORY: Visitors see a real room and a real running order, then the posters and guests prove it recurs. They join Telegram and know where Carmel Kitchen is.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink. Left 7/12: ግራጫ ቃጭሎች at display scale over the looping room video in a rounded panel. Right 5/12: the programme heading, then each part (discussion, members read, songs) on its own 2px rule. Across the foot of the hero runs the posters' info strip: time | place with drawn icons, then the terracotta Join on Telegram stamp.

FORM: The Night's Programme, grounded candidate 6 of 7, seed fd3763fe. Raises: the programme is the page's one unbroken line (Drum Machine Step Row). No enclosing boxes; text rides rules and baselines (Cracktro Scroller).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
